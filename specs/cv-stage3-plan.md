## Goal and posture

Stage 3 closes the EFT direction's remaining open clause: **interactions**. The posture is the
chain's standing one — everything at a finite cutoff (`K` modes, `N` levels), no continuum
claims (`ApproxCCR.no_exact_finite_ccr` is why), and the honest finite statements chosen so
they *survive* the continuum limit as the standard axioms. The stage-3 shape mirrors what CV-5/
CV-6 did for the free field: build the object explicitly, prove the exp-legitimacy theorem, and
make locality a theorem about the dynamics.

The physics content, in one line each:

1. an **interacting drive** exists and is generated by its Hamiltonian (3a);
2. interaction is exactly what *spreads* operator support — bounded by a **light cone** on the
   coupling graph, one edge per period (3b);
3. the locality violation is **priced linearly in the coupling** (3c, the Duhamel bound);
4. the price **scales with the cutoff** by operator dimension — finite-cutoff power counting,
   the honest kernel of relevant/irrelevant (3d).

## Stage 3b — local algebras and the light cone (M/L) — row CV-8 ★★ the marquee

New module `CV/LocalAlgebra.lean` + `CV/SupportSpreading.lean`.

**(i) `SupportedOn` is a unital *-subalgebra** (checked on paper both ways 2026-08-07; the
`indep` cases need the same offDiag-kills-both rescue as the CV-6 union argument):
`SupportedOn.one`, `.add`, `.smul`, `.mul` (intermediate-config bijection `e ↦ (e on T, c' off T)`),
`.star`, and `.mono : S ⊆ S' → SupportedOn S A → SupportedOn S' A` (case-split: a moved mode in
`S' \ S` zeroes both entries; else the S-spectators are unmoved). This is the Haag–Kastler
**local algebra** at the cutoff — library-grade API-first per CONVENTIONS §9.

**(ii) Conjugation spreading bound.** For any *unitary* `U` with `SupportedOn T U.val`:
`heisenberg_supportedOn_union : SupportedOn S A → SupportedOn (S ∪ T) (heisenberg U A)` — a
three-line corollary of (i) (`star`, `mul`, `mono`). The CV-6 diagonal-phase lemma becomes an
instance. Sharpening: if `Disjoint S T` then `heisenberg U A = A` (disjoint algebras commute by
`commute_of_disjointSupport`, so conjugation is trivial) — **support only grows into modes the
interaction actually couples**.

**(iii) The light cone.** For a kicked drive `freeFieldU · kick₁ · … · kickₘ` with each kick
supported on an edge `{kᵢ, lᵢ}` of a coupling graph `G`:
★★ `support_subset_ball_of_pow : SupportedOn S A → SupportedOn (ball_G S n) (heisenberg (U^n) A)`
— **information propagates at most one graph edge per period**. Induction from (ii) + the CV-6
free-part invariance; `ball_G S n` = the n-fold `Finset` neighbourhood (small graph-combinatorics
helper, no Mathlib graph theory needed — `Finset.biUnion` of edge endpoints).

**(iv) Non-vacuity witness** (the converse of CV-6, and what retires the CV-6 scope boundary):
at `K = N = 2`, a concrete density kick and `modeOp 0 a` whose Heisenberg image **violates**
`SupportedOn {0}` (`indep` fails: the conjugating phase `c₂(c₁ − d₁)`-dependence is explicit) —
finite `decide`/`norm_num` computation in the `kickedIsing_changes_marginal` style. Statement:
`¬ SupportedOn {0} (heisenberg pairKick (modeOp 0 a))` for the exhibited `a`, kick angle.

Ledger action on landing: the CV-6 boundary ("interacting drives can spread support — not
claimed here") in `DynamicalLocality.lean` + the `AxiomAudit/Extensions.lean` pin comment is
**superseded at source** — spreading becomes a *theorem with a bound*, not an open scope. Re-read

  invariance + submultiplicativity of the C* norm) ⇒ the evolved observable is within
  `2τ|λ|·sup|v|·‖A‖` of the *support-preserved* free-evolved one: **locality violation priced
  linearly in the coupling** — the CV rhyme of the record half-life bound `≤ n·ε`.
- Optional diagnostics tie-in (only if S-sized when reached): first-period Loschmidt bound

## Stage 3d — finite-cutoff power counting (L, research-flagged) — row CV-10

Decision point AFTER 3a–3c land; do not start without a fresh feasibility pass (per the
"check impossible before scoped" rule — the claims below were sanity-checked but not
proof-sketched to the bottom).

The honest finite content of "power counting": grade interactions by operator content and track
how the 3c price **scales with the cutoff** `N`:

- `‖Q‖ = O(√N)` bricks (position/ladder operator norm growth; upper bound suffices — Frobenius
  or row-sum route, no spectral asymptotics).
- For a graded interaction `V = Q_k^m`-type: price `≤ τ|λ|·C·N^{m/2}` ⇒ the coupling must be
  renormalized like `λ(N) ~ N^{−m/2}` for a cutoff-stable effect — **relevant/irrelevant as a
  theorem about cutoff scaling of the Duhamel price**, no RG flow claimed.
- Cutoff-independence complement: density couplings built from occupation numbers have
  `N`-independent matrix elements on fixed low configurations (`fieldEnergy_cutoff_independent`
  lineage) — the "relevant" side.

Honest scope to write into the module doc on day one: this is power counting of *price bounds*
at a finite cutoff; no continuum limit, no renormalization-group fixed points, no
non-perturbative claims.
